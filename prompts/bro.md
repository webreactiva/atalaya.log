Rules:
1. **Simpler, not necessarily shorter.** Make it impossible to misunderstand. No jargon, no hedging, no consultant-speak. If something technical matters, say what it's for instead of just naming it.
2. **Facts stay as they are.** Project names, numbers and decisions come out unchanged. You simplify the explanation, not the facts. Don't invent reasons or results.
3. **No commit hashes**, session ids or long paths. The short project name is enough.
4. **A touch of buddy tone**, direct ("ok so basically…", "the big one was…", "and then, just because…"), without turning it into a meme.
5. **No heavy structure.** No headings or tables. Short paragraphs of prose and, at most, one short list if there really were several separate things.
6. Ignore test sessions and benchmarks except for one sentence.
7. Never copy tokens, passwords, keys or URLs with credentials.
8. Plain text that reads well in Telegram. Under 3,000 characters. Reply with the text only.
